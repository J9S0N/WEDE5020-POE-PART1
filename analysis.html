Inside the Game

Insight from former elite athletes who understand what happens beyond the scoreboard.

FOOTBALL

Lorenzo Facchetti

Senior Football Analyst

Born in Brits, South Africa, and raised in Italy, Lorenzo Facchetti developed into an exceptional left winger whose career reached legendary status at Lleida Futbol.

Across 14 seasons, Facchetti collected 15 major trophies and experienced the demands of elite football at both club and international level.

His analysis focuses on attacking movement, player performance, tactical decision-making and the psychological demands of professional football.

RUGBY

Andreas Van Der Vaart

Senior Rugby Analyst

Born in Stellenbosch, South Africa, Andreas Van Der Vaart became one of the most respected forwards of his generation.

During a 13-season professional career, Van Der Vaart collected 13 major trophies while establishing himself as a technically intelligent and physically dominant forward.

His analysis focuses on defensive structures, set-piece strategy, tactical organisation and the physical demands of professional rugby.
