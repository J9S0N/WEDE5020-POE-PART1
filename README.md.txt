Project Overview

The Medalist is a fictional digital sports publication providing
South African and international sports coverage.

The publication focuses primarily on football, with rugby as its
secondary sport, as well as cricket and basketball.

The central philosophy of The Medalist is:

"The Human Within the Extraordinary"

The website aims to look beyond sporting performances by providing
expert analysis, athlete profiles, documentaries, behind-the-scenes
stories and coverage of emerging sporting talent.



Website Goals and Objectives

The website aims to:

- Provide accessible South African and international sports coverage.
- Provide expert analysis from former high-performing athletes.
- Highlight emerging sporting talent.
- Publish human-interest stories and documentaries.
- Provide behind-the-scenes perspectives on athletes and teams.
- Allow visitors to contact The Medalist and submit enquiries.



Key Features and Functionality

- Responsive website layout
