About The Medalist

The Medalist is a sports journalism publication built around one simple belief: behind every extraordinary sporting achievement is a human being.

The Human Within the Extraordinary

The Medalist was founded by an athlete and journalist who grew up together dreaming of representing South Africa at the World Cup.

While one pursued the dream of becoming a professional athlete, the other found a new path through sports journalism. Through their experiences, they saw how the pressure to perform and the constant attention surrounding athletes could cause the person behind the performance to be forgotten.

The Medalist was created to provide sports coverage that goes beyond scores and statistics. Our stories explore the people, experiences and challenges behind sporting achievements.

What We Cover

Our publication covers football, rugby, cricket and basketball through news, expert analysis, documentaries, behind-the-scenes stories and coverage of emerging local talent.

Our analysts also bring experience from high-level sport, providing readers with informed perspectives on the game.
